\subsection{Document-Level Reproducibility Protocol}
The controlled evaluation uses 52 complete CTI reports assigned to five
mutually exclusive document folds with split seed 11800. Fold sizes are 11,
11, 10, 10, and 10 reports. All content and annotations from one report remain
within a single outer fold. In outer iteration $k$, fold $k$ is test data;
validation documents are drawn only from the remaining outer-training
partition. Model checkpoints, decoding choices, reconciliation policies, and
thresholds are selected using validation data and frozen before test scoring.

The contextual model uses a \texttt{roberta-base} encoder, a two-layer BiGRU
with hidden size 250, eight attention heads, and dropout 0.35. Encoder and task
head learning rates are $3\times10^{-5}$ and $3\times10^{-4}$, respectively,
with weight decay 0.01. Joint training uses at most 30 epochs with patience 5;
relation refinement uses at most 12 epochs with patience 4. Entity
boundary/type prediction uses 61 BIEOS sequence tags. Relation roles are
predicted by a separate four-class head: \texttt{O}, \texttt{ROLE\_1},
\texttt{ROLE\_2}, and \texttt{ROLE\_BOTH}.
